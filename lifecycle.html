The full path

Know what each stage means.

Creating an order does not put an inscription on-chain. The Bitcoin payment begins the commit-and-reveal sequence.

1. Order created

The action is turned into an exact inscription payload. A unique commit address, minimum amount, receiver, and payment window are shown.

2. Details reviewed

You compare the payload and destination with your intent and save the order information.

3. Commit funded

You send one dedicated Bitcoin output to the displayed address. Smaller outputs are not combined.

4. Payment detected

The order recognizes an eligible output at the commit address and begins the reveal.

5. Inscription revealed

The commit output is spent into the reveal transaction, which sends the inscription to the selected receiver.

6. Result verified

You inspect the reveal on Bitcoin and use a compatible reader to understand the BLOCK-20 state.

Order signals

Read the status without guessing.

Keep the order open after payment. Some stages need time to reach the next Bitcoin transaction.

Awaiting payment

No eligible commit payment has been used. Confirm your wallet transaction and wait for detection.

Revealed

A reveal transaction is available. Open it and compare the inscription with your preview.

Reveal retrying

The reveal did not complete on its first attempt. Keep the order record and do not pay again.

Needs help

The order could not complete automatically. Preserve the order and payment details for support.

Expired

The displayed payment window passed without an eligible payment being detected.

Do not pay twice. A slow status update is not a new payment request. Check your wallet transaction and order details first. If the order needs help, preserve the evidence and contact the service you used.

Payment safety

Keep the commit address single-purpose.

Send one output at or above the displayed minimum. Do not split the amount, add unrelated funds, reuse an old commit address, or change the receiver after the order is created. If anything is wrong before payment, create a fresh order.

Save before sending

Keep the payload, receiver, order identity, commit address, minimum amount, expiry, and fee together.

Save after sending

Keep the wallet transaction ID, then add the reveal transaction ID when it appears.
