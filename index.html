How it works

From idea to inscription in three clear steps.

You choose the token action and receiver. Universe Inscribe prepares the order, watches for its dedicated payment, and completes the reveal.

01

Create your action

Pick deploy, mint, or transfer. Enter the ticker and amount or supply details, then choose your receiving address.

02

Approve the details

Review the compact inscription, receiving address, one-time commit address, required amount, expiry, and fee.

03

Fund and verify

Send one dedicated Bitcoin output. Follow the order until the reveal transaction appears, then inspect it on-chain.
