How it works

From intent to inscription in three clear steps.

BLOCK-20 handles the commit and reveal workflow behind a simple, visible path. You stay in control of the action, destination, and payment review.

01

Create your action

Pick deploy, mint, or transfer. Enter the ticker and details, then choose the Bitcoin address that should receive the inscription.

02

Approve what goes on-chain

Review the exact inscription payload, the generated commit address, the required Bitcoin amount, and the current fee rate.

03

Fund and follow the reveal

Send one dedicated Bitcoin output. Once it is detected, the service reveals the inscription and returns a transaction ID to verify.

Designed for a clear handoff

Know what you are funding before you fund it.

A BLOCK-20 order makes the important moments visible: your receiving address, the compact inscription JSON, the exact commit address, and the amount required to continue.

Payload

Exact bytes, visible first

The order returns the exact JSON that will be inscribed, so you do not need to reconstruct it after payment.

Payment

One intentional output

Fund the returned address with one dedicated Bitcoin output at or above the displayed amount.

Verification

Reveal, then check your reader

Use the returned reveal transaction and a compatible reader or indexer to inspect the final result.
