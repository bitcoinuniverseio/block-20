Choose

Start with the right action.

Each action creates a different inscription. Read the preview and make sure its meaning matches your intent.

Deploy

Define a ticker

Choose the ticker, maximum supply, mint limit, and optional public description. Check spelling and capitalization before creating the order.

Mint

Create an amount

Choose an existing ticker and amount. The order adds the Bitcoin block reference used by the mint inscription.

Transfer

Prepare movement

Choose the ticker and amount, then send the revealed inscription to the intended Bitcoin address.

Review

Pause at the order screen.

This is the moment to catch a mistake. Do not open your wallet until every visible detail is right.

Confirm the inscription

  • The operation matches deploy, mint, or transfer.
  • The ticker, amount, supply, limit, and description match your intent.
  • A mint includes the displayed Bitcoin block hash.

Confirm the payment

  • The receiver is a Bitcoin address you control.
  • The commit address and minimum amount match the open order.
  • The fee and payment window are acceptable to you.
Never share wallet secrets. BLOCK-20 does not need your seed phrase or private key. Your wallet should only be asked to send the reviewed Bitcoin amount to the order's displayed commit address.

Fund

Send one dedicated output, once.

Use the exact commit address and at least the minimum amount shown. Do not split the amount into smaller payments, reuse the address for another purpose, or send a second payment because the page takes time to update.

Keep the order page and payment record. Save the order identity, inscription preview, receiver, commit address, amount, expiry, and wallet transaction ID until the reveal is complete.

Verify

Follow the reveal all the way to Bitcoin.

After the payment is detected, the order moves toward reveal. When a reveal transaction appears, open it in a Bitcoin explorer and compare the inscription with your saved preview. Then use a compatible BLOCK-20 reader to see the token result.
